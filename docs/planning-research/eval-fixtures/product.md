# Task

Plan a small experiment to decide whether to launch personalized onboarding
for our collaboration product. We have two weeks and one product engineer.
Give a concise recommendation with success criteria and the decisions we need
to make. No access to users, analytics tools, or web research in this exercise;
use the supplied evidence and mark gaps. Do not implement or contact anyone.

# Supplied evidence

- Six interviewees said the first session felt confusing. All six were recruited
  from customers who had already contacted support.
- A dashboard screenshot copied into notes reports 31% activation. Its activation
  definition and denominator are not recorded; raw data is unavailable.
- A designer proposes personalized checklists for three personas. The personas
  came from an internal workshop, not customer segmentation research.
- The existing onboarding has one checklist shown to everyone.
- The team can instrument events and change checklist copy in two weeks. Building
  a personalization rules service would probably exceed that window.
- The business wants improved activation without increasing support load. There
  is no agreed minimum worthwhile improvement or experiment sample size yet.
