# Task

Review the plan below and recommend a concise research approach for deciding
whether a four-day workweek improves retention in our company. Use only the
supplied evidence; browsing and independent agents are unavailable in this
exercise. Do not change this input or invent additional studies. Include a
diagram if it reveals a consequential relationship.

# Evidence packet

- Source A: a vendor-authored case study reports 20% lower voluntary turnover
  after a four-day-week rollout at one 80-person company. No comparison group,
  raw counts, or definition of the reporting period is provided.
- Source B: a newsletter repeats Source A's 20% claim and links only to A.
- Source C: an internal spreadsheet shows six resignations among 100 employees
  in the prior six months, and four among 110 employees in the next six months.
  Compensation bands and managers also changed between periods. No policy
  experiment was conducted.
- Source D: an internal interview memo says several employees value schedule
  flexibility more than fewer working days. Sampling details are missing.

# Existing plan

1. Treat A and B as independent confirmations of the retention benefit.
2. Attribute the internal reduction from six to four resignations to a shorter
   week and project that effect onto the whole company.
3. Run a four-week trial; declare success if annual retention improves 20%.
4. Recommend company-wide adoption after the four-week result.
