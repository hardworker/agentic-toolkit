# Reading and grading the concrete-handoff outputs

Keep this file separate from authoring inputs. Freeze it before inspecting outputs. Assess the artifacts against the request and source packet, not keyword or heading compliance. No preferred runtime, schema, section count or output length is prescribed. A different mechanism is acceptable if supported and consistent with the requirements.

For each substantial engineering output, first answer as a new implementer:

1. What result is intended, and which scope/constraints are binding?
2. What runs the work, how do responsibilities interact, and which choices remain open?
3. How does an input become a context-backed decision or draft? Explain the meaning and ownership of information crossing relevant boundaries.
4. What happens on repeated/new evidence, access loss, ambiguity, exhausted budget and processing/delivery failure?
5. What is the first independently assessable result, what precedes it, and how would it be demonstrated?
6. Can the next delivery proceed without inventing a central architectural decision? Distinguish a proposed design from verified capabilities.

Record each answer as supported, partial, missing or contradicted, with a specific passage or absent behavior and its consequence. Do not require an exact implementation, production thresholds without evidence or exhaustive test cases. Distinguish central omissions from reasonable details left to implementation.

## Task-specific checks

- The complete design retains repository activity, inbox and feedback despite staging. Individual draft approval, current audience authorization, quiet hours, bounded spend and inspectable failures remain requirements.
- Exact-delivery deduplication is different from a later revision or recurrence. Correlation uses evidence rather than assuming similar text means the same work. Source restrictions apply to information used, including derived content, not just displayed citations.
- Runtime choice and host enforcement must be assessable against the supplied capabilities. An unsupported preferred capability stays conditional; adding an unnecessary platform is a cost requiring justification.
- In the later update, inbox becomes the first slice without dropping other source types. Estimated delivery cost is distinct from actual investigation spend. Deletion invalidates pending drafts and is addressed across preparation, approval and delivery, not only at intake. The current design, sequence and acceptance agree; superseded decisions remain identifiable outside the current design.
- The small review preserves the supplied plan unless it identifies a real problem, with no unnecessary interview, architecture or implementation work.
- The investigation uses the primary 30-day question, handles incomplete cohorts/event coverage, separates evidence from hypotheses and defines a concrete two-week investigation and decision. It preserves opt-in access, the gamification exclusion and the lack of evidence for a chosen feature. Do not require a particular sample size or research design if the reasoning is sound.
- The readiness review preserves the draft, identifies why the complete design is not ready, and distinguishes independently ready intake. It must not substitute a narrower prototype assignment or invent missing facts.

## Usability and comparison

Assess whether design decisions, rationale, first delivery and acceptance are findable; identify unnecessary repetition, process or code prescription that actually makes the handoff harder to use. Length is descriptive, not a quality score. Record both strengths and defects for each method, including an inconclusive result or a community advantage. Do not infer general superiority from one run. Compare the whole engineering artifact set if a method uses multiple files.

The reader is given anonymized output labels and the original source packets, not authoring instructions or method names. Formatting may still reveal a method; describe this as label-blinded reading, not assured blinding. A later parent audit checks consequential findings against raw outputs and sources. No grade establishes implementation correctness.
