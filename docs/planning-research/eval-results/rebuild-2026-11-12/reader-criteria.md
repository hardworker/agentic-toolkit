# Reader brief for the rebuilt planning trial

Read the supplied user request and source packet, the private reference handoff, and the two candidate engineering documents. The reference is a quality/structure preference, not a source of requirements for the synthetic system. Do not import its domain-specific architecture or demand its section count or length. Do not quote its private project details in the report.

Compare the documents as a person who needs to understand the proposal, challenge its design and start the next delivery. Judge:
- Whether the recommendation, present foundation/gaps, main design concepts and path to delivery are easy to find and form one coherent explanation.
- Whether each important concept is explained with meaningful rules, interactions and rationale, rather than being named or packed into an overloaded paragraph.
- Whether stages identify useful results, deliverables, dependencies and exit evidence; the first slice should be clear without losing the broader design.
- Whether examples, diagrams and data shapes clarify real decisions, rather than merely adding volume.
- Whether assumptions, missing capabilities, unnecessary mechanisms or contradictory acceptance undermine the proposed handoff.

Identify concrete strengths and defects with passages. Do not score headings, length or keyword coverage. A preference is optional, task-specific and not proof of user satisfaction. No output is expected to be perfect. Distinguish a central design gap from a safe implementation choice. Say if a difference is mostly presentation.

For the rebuilt method's controls, check only that the small review stays small and preserves the supplied plan, the research plan has concrete methods/decisions without choosing a feature, and the readiness review preserves the draft and assesses the full requested scope. These are breadth checks, not a second comparative benchmark.

Do not inspect authoring instructions, method identities, research rationale, prior graders or other agents. Use only the given files. No implementation, external actions, browsing or new agents. Output a compact reader report in the assigned scratch location. This is document inspection, not validation of runtime behavior.
